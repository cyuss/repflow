RepFlow is a flexible strength-training app for Garmin watches.

Change exercise order at any time, skip occupied machines, resume exercises
later and track your sets, reps and weights without interrupting your workout.

Your workout. Your order.

WHY REPFLOW
Structured workouts assume you will do the exercises in the order they were
written. Real gyms disagree: the machine is taken, you want to superset, you
want to come back to something later. RepFlow lets you move freely without
losing a single set.

ON THE GYM FLOOR
- Select any exercise at any time from a one-press workout overview
- "Skip for now" marks an occupied machine as pending, never as completed or
  skipped, and it resumes exactly where you left it
- Substitute an exercise when the machine is taken for good — the sets you
  already did are kept, not deleted
- Add an unplanned exercise, or one more set than you planned, mid-session
- The next set is pre-filled with the weight and reps you actually performed
- One press of START completes a set, then you correct the reps if they came
  out differently

BUILD YOUR OWN, ON THE WATCH
- 82 movements across ten muscle groups
- Start from an empty workout and add exercises as you go
- Edit sets, reps, load, rest and order without touching a phone
- Custom workouts name themselves after what is in them

WHAT YOU GET BACK
- Last time's numbers for every movement, before you decide the load
- Personal records — heaviest set, best estimated maximum, best volume — with
  their own alert the moment one falls
- Weekly training volume per muscle group, so you can see what you are
  neglecting
- Live heart rate with a five-segment zone gauge, and time in zone at the end
- Heart rate recovery between sets
- Body Battery before and after the session
- Optional automatic rep counting from the wrist, which suggests a number and
  never logs one on its own

DETAILS
- Records a real strength training activity that syncs to Garmin Connect, with
  one lap per set carrying the exercise, reps and load
- Kilograms or pounds, following your watch, with an override in the settings
- Works fully offline: no phone, no internet, no account
- Survives an interrupted workout: your session is restored with every set

PLEASE NOTE
Garmin's Connect IQ platform does not allow third-party apps to write native
per-set data into the FIT file. RepFlow records a genuine strength training
activity and marks one lap per set, and keeps the full set-by-set detail on the
watch. Garmin Connect's built-in per-set breakdown will not be populated, and
the session does not contribute to Training Effect or recovery time, which the
watch computes itself and does not expose to apps.
