v1.0.0 — first release

- Select any exercise at any time. Skip an occupied machine, come back to it
  later with every set intact.
- Build and edit workouts on the watch: 82 movements across ten muscle groups,
  no phone needed.
- Start from an empty workout and add exercises as you go.
- Add, substitute or add a set to any exercise mid-session.
- Personal records, with their own alert the moment one falls.
- Weekly training volume per muscle group.
- Live heart rate with a zone gauge, and time in zone at the end.
- Heart rate recovery between sets, and Body Battery before and after.
- Optional automatic rep counting from the wrist.
- Kilograms or pounds, following your watch.
